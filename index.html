MetabolomicsPractical Guide to Design and Analysis

Edited by Ron Wehrens and Reza Salek

First edition · Chapman & Hall/CRC · 2019

A practical guide to planning metabolomics studies, choosing measurement methods, and processing and interpreting the resulting data. The chapters grew out of EMBO practical courses in metabolomics bioinformatics.

View at the publisher ↗

What the book covers

The book follows a study from experimental design through measurement, data processing and analysis. It also covers statistics, combining datasets, metabolic networks and flux, and standards for sharing data. Worked examples and exercises help readers put these methods into practice.
